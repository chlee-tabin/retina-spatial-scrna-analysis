# Figure 4 Reconstruction of spatial expression from chick scRNA-seq dataset

(A) UMAP plot of major cell clusters from chick E5-6 retinal samples. (B) Violin plot of marker gene expression across the graph-based clusters (leiden resolution=0.1) (C) Spatial expression pattern of select genes along the DV.Score (Dorso-Ventral score). (D) Expression of select genes along the NT.Score (Naso-Temporal score) (E) Topographic representation of gene expression of select markers. The bottom right present the percentile clip. Max means the gene maximum expression level of which the viridis color scale is normalized. (F) 20 Spatial patterns (anchors) that maximize the divergence of spatial expression pattern in chick scRNA-seq datasets. Anchors that have mutual neighbors were highlighted with the number of genes associated with the cluster. A yellow label indicate manual annotation of certain clusters.

# Figure S4 

(A) UMAP plot depiction of Phase (top left), 10X Chromium technology (top right), mitochondrial content (bottom left), gene count per cell (bottom right). (B1) Violin plot of W chromosome and Z chromosome gene fraction per cell for inferred individual embryos from all libraries. Note that avian transcriptomes do not display sex chromosome dosage compensation such that females (WZ) have half the Z gene expression than males (ZZ), and males do not have W gene expression. (B2) Composition of the individual embryos of cell types. (C0) Distribution of Dosal, Ventral score and the subtracted composite score DV.Score. (C1) Distribution of Dorsal and Ventral scores. Cells represent single-cell transcriptomes. (C2) Distribution of gene expression along the DV.Score axis. The grayed out genes represent the genes used for the DV score construction. (C3) Binned expression pattern along the DV.Score axis, with individual points representing pseudobulked expression from individual embryos. (D0) Distribution of Nasal, Temporal score and the subtracted composite score NT.Score. (D1) Distribution of Nasal, Temporal scores. Cells represent single-cell transcriptomes. (D2) Distribution of gene expression along the NT.Score axis. The grayed out genes represent the genes used for the NT score construction. (D3) Binned expression pattern along the NT.Score axis, with individual points representing pseudobulked expression from individual embryos. (E fovea) Virtual selection of the CYP26C1 area. The topographic gene expression of CYP26C1 was overlayed with area bins, overlayed with blue area (four 2D spatial bins) that were marked as "fovea" area. (E nasal) Virtual selection of the nasal most area. The topographic gene expression of FOXG1 was overlayed with area bins, the selected area overlayed with blue. (E temporal) Virtual selection of the temporal most area. The topographic gene expression of FOXD1 was overlayed with area bins, with selected area overlayed with blue. (E area) Volcano plot using pseudobulked gene expression samples that are enriched or de-enriched in the fovea, nasal, and temporal area. Points are scaled by average gene expression level, and genes above 2-fold expression difference between in-group and out-group and adjusted p-values less than 0.05 were colored. The top 5 enriched genes without the "LOC" prefix were highlighted. See also Supplemental Table 0.
